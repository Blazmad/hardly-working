Hardly Working

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The app

Hardly Working collects nothing. It makes no network connection, has no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. It stores two settings on your Mac, in its own preferences: whether it is active, and the idle delay you picked. Launch at login is handled by macOS itself.

It asks for the Accessibility permission because macOS requires it to move the cursor. It uses it only to post mouse-move events. It never reads your keyboard, your screen, your files or other apps. The source code is public, so you can check all of this: github.com/Blazmad/hardly-working.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to run and secure the service, under its own privacy statement.

Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data. It records the page, the referring site, the browser, the operating system, the screen size and the country, in aggregate. See its privacy policy.

Downloads

The app is downloaded from GitHub Releases or installed with Homebrew. Those services apply their own privacy policies.
