What it actually does

01

It moves the cursor by one pixel. That's the entire crime.

Nothing opens, nothing gets selected, nothing you'd have to explain.

02

It tells you when it stops working.

Most jigglers just quietly die. You find out when someone asks where you were all afternoon.

03

It waits until you've actually left.

While you're using your Mac, it does nothing at all. No cursor fighting, no jumping.

The part nobody else mentions

What we're not hiding from you

Your Mac decides to sleep by watching the same idle counter this app keeps resetting. So while Hardly Working is active, your Mac won't fall asleep on its own — and neither will your screen lock itself.

On a work machine that matters, precisely in the situation this app is built for. Lock it yourself on the way out: Ctrl ⌘ Q, or set a hot corner to lock the screen.

Every other jiggler does this too. We're just the ones telling you.
